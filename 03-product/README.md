# 03 — Product Definition (FIXGO)

> **What is this?** The answer to "what are we going to build?" for FIXGO. It defines the validated problem, product vision, strategic pillars, and delivery roadmap.

## Why this section exists

Without a clear product definition, teams build unwanted features and scope creeps out of control. This section acts as the official contract between the development team and academic reviewers regarding what FIXGO will achieve and why.

---

## What is inside this folder

### `problem-framing.md` ⭐
Articulates the core problem before proposing technical solutions. Captures driver pain points, road emergency contexts, and current inefficiencies.

### `vision.md` ⭐
The product's north star, mission statement, strategic pillars (Speed, Reliability, Security), product principles, and high-level delivery roadmap spanning Sprints 1 through 6.
